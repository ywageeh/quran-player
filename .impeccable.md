## Design Context

### Users
Muslims listening to or memorizing Quran ayahs — choosing a reciter, a surah range, and repetition counts for focused practice.

### Brand Personality
Calm, reverent, focused. Quiet confidence without ornament for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light stone and soft sage surfaces with deep emerald ink. Arabic-first RTL. Calligraphic Quran display (Amiri) with clean UI type (IBM Plex Sans Arabic). Soft geometric atmosphere — not cream/terracotta, not purple glow, not dark neon.

### Design Principles
1. Arabic is the primary language of the interface.
2. Controls stay simple: select, set repeats, play.
3. The current ayah is the visual center while listening.
4. Motion is gentle and purposeful — entrances and play-state only.
5. Respect and clarity over decorative excess.
